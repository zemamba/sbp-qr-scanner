How does it work?

The page scans a QR code with the camera and shows its contents. For SBP QR codes (qr.nspk.ru), the payment link is handed to the selected banking app. The bank app requests payment details from NSPK, shows the recipient and amount, and asks you to confirm the payment inside the bank app. On Android Chrome, the handoff uses intent links for supported banks. In other browsers, the system app picker opens the payment link. GOST payment details are shown for copying because banking apps usually do not accept them through an external deep link. Everything runs locally: camera frames and scanned data are not sent to this project or to any custom server.
